# Spine Pair Review - Agentic Story Mapper

Reviewed: `DESIGN.md` and `EXPERIENCE.md` (status draft, 2026-09-29) against the PRD, `epics.md`, the architecture spine and `apps/web/src` (read-only). Contrast ratios recomputed with the WCAG 2.x relative-luminance formula.

## Overall verdict

Adequate as a contract, not yet safe to consume as-is. The mechanical layer is clean: every `{token}` reference resolves, all 46 colors carry hex, the UJ titles and requirement numbers are verbatim, the section order is canonical, the six in-scope journeys have named protagonists, numbered steps, a climax and a failure path, and every story number in the "Existing GUI" table exists in `epics.md`. The weight of the risk sits in three places: the highest-risk new surfaces (Slices, Graph, ChangeSet review, What changed, drift drawer, restore) have behavior but almost no visual component rows, several "ratified existing" claims do not match the code, and the WCAG 2.1 AA target is contradicted by the spine's own rules (reflow, single-key shortcuts, color-only swatch). 0 critical, 5 high, 39 medium and 21 low findings.

UJ-6 exclusion is defensible: its path is repo docs, CI and a pull request, with no product surface (FR-40 to FR-45, FR-55 are non-UI); the one-line exclusion note is the right size.

## 2. Token completeness - adequate

**What was checked:** all frontmatter tokens and every `{path.to.token}` in DESIGN.md prose and in EXPERIENCE.md; hex on all colors; stated contrast claims recomputed; use of tokens by components.

Result: all references resolve (colors, typography, rounded, spacing 1 to 6, components). Recomputed ratios on white unless noted: `primary` 7.41 (stated "about 7.6"), `primary-hover` 4.97 (stated 4.9, ok; 4.62 on `background`), `chrome-text` on `chrome` 10.13 (stated "about 9"), `text` 15.46 and `text-muted` 5.52 on `background`, all four marker colors 5.64 to 7.41 on white and 4.52 to 6.92 on the level tints, `focus-ring` 4.97 on white and exactly 3.00 on `chrome`. All semantic and actor text/surface pairs pass 4.5 (7.15 to 12.83).

### Findings

- **[medium]** Two stated ratios are wrong: `primary` on white is 7.41 not "about 7.6", `chrome-text` on `chrome` is 10.13 not "about 9". Both errors are conservative, so no pair fails, but the "computed by hand" note admits they were not checked (DESIGN.md Colors, Contrast paragraph). *Fix:* correct the numbers or drop them and rely on the Story 6.10 axe run.
- **[medium]** No non-text contrast target. Borders are 1.27 to 1.51:1 on white (`border` 1.37, `border-strong` 1.51), so input and select boundaries fail WCAG 1.4.11 (3:1) under the stated 2.1 AA target; Card color swatches are 1.0 to 1.46:1 against white (DESIGN.md Colors; global.css `input, select, textarea`). *Fix:* add a `border-input` token at 3:1 or higher and state the 3:1 rule for controls and swatches.
- **[medium]** The Card color category hexes and the priority-active orange live only in `card-visual-options.ts` and `.card-priority-badge.has-priority`, outside the token set, so the palette a consumer must implement cannot be source-extracted. White text on the priority orange `#F5A623` is 2.03:1, contradicting "all text meets 4.5:1" (DESIGN.md Colors, "Card color categories" bullet). *Fix:* tokenise `category-*` and `priority-*` and fix the priority text color.
- **[medium]** No interaction-state tokens. `primary-hover` exists but no component references it; `button-primary` has no hover, disabled or focus entry; disabled today is `opacity: .55`, which drops text below 4.5:1; the bulk-selected tile state (`surface-selected` fill plus `primary` border in global.css) is unused by any component token (DESIGN.md frontmatter `components`). *Fix:* add hover, disabled and selected variants for button, tab, tile and input.
- **[medium]** `danger` (`#B3403F`) has no consumer and there is no destructive button, yet Confirm dialogs require a primary button repeating the verb ("Delete story", "Restore") (EXPERIENCE Component Patterns, Confirm dialog; DESIGN frontmatter). *Fix:* add `button-danger` and state when it is used.
- **[medium]** Elevation and motion have no values. Shadows are "small" and "large" with no tokens, while the code uses `0 4px 12px` and `0 20px 60px`; the "brief highlight", "pulse once" and "short transitions" have no duration, easing or highlight color (DESIGN.md Elevation & Depth, Brand & Style; EXPERIENCE Interaction Primitives, Live updates). *Fix:* add `shadow-popover`, `shadow-modal`, `motion-highlight` (duration, color) tokens.
- **[medium]** No `actor-system` token or badge, although the Activity Log and FR-47 carry a fourth actor type (system: migration, restore, pre-apply) and Flow 5 filters by actor type (DESIGN Components, Actor badge; PRD glossary Activity Log). *Fix:* add `actor-system` colors and a gear-icon badge.
- **[low]** Several hex values are reused under different meanings: `level-step`, `warning-surface` and `warning-border` equal the Step tile amber; `primary`, `actor-human`, `marker-added` and `success-text` are one green; the Risk swatch and priority orange are one amber. A warning callout beside Step tiles reads as a tile (DESIGN frontmatter colors). *Fix:* shift `warning-*` toward orange or note the overlap as accepted.
- **[low]** Tokens with no consumer in prose or components: `surface-muted`, `surface-selected`, `border-subtle`, `text` (DESIGN frontmatter). *Fix:* tie each to a component or remove it.
- **[low]** Marker colors are verified "on white" but the marker sits on the tile top edge over tinted tiles; the minimum is 4.52 (`marker-removed` on the Activity tint), passing with no margin (DESIGN.md Components, Change marker). *Fix:* state the verified backgrounds.

## 8. Shape fit - adequate

**What was checked:** DESIGN.md order against the spec; EXPERIENCE default sections; dropped and invented sections; Accessibility Floor and Interaction Primitives content against the stated WCAG 2.1 AA target.

Result: DESIGN.md follows the canonical order (Brand & Style, Colors, Typography, Layout & Spacing, Elevation & Depth, Shapes, Components, Do's and Don'ts). EXPERIENCE has all eight defaults; Responsive & Platform matches the shadcn example; Existing GUI and Open Items are invented and justified for a brownfield spine; Inspiration & Anti-patterns is dropped with a stated reason and its content is partly carried by Do's and Don'ts and "Banned everywhere". The accessibility content is where the shape hides real defects.

### Findings

- **[high]** The WCAG 2.1 AA target is contradicted by the responsive rule: below 768px is "untested" and 200 percent zoom on a 1280px window is a 640px viewport, while 1.4.10 Reflow requires usability at 320 CSS px (400 percent at 1280px) apart from two-dimensional content such as the board (EXPERIENCE Responsive & Platform, Accessibility Floor last bullet). *Fix:* state that non-board views and panels reflow to 320 CSS px, or downgrade the claim to "2.1 AA except reflow" in the assumption.
- **[high]** Single-character shortcuts break WCAG 2.1.4 (Level A): `/` is global and `m` and `Delete` act on tiles with no off or remap; `Space` toggles bulk select but is also button activation inside a tile (EXPERIENCE Interaction Primitives, Keyboard). *Fix:* scope every key to a focused board, add a way to disable, and define the `Space` conflict.
- **[medium]** A roving-focus grid conflicts with composite tiles that contain a checkbox, up to six icon buttons, a swatch, a priority control and a content button: how focus enters and leaves a tile, and which key opens the Card, is undefined (EXPERIENCE Interaction Primitives; Accessibility Floor, tile name `{Card Key} {level} {title}`; CardTile.tsx). *Fix:* define one tab stop per tile with `Enter` to open, `F2` or arrow-into for actions, or a toolbar pattern.
- **[medium]** The Card color category is the only carrier on the tile: the swatch has no visible word and its accessible name is the static `aria-label` "Farbkategorie wählen"; the "text label" exists only in popover, modal and filters, contradicting "never color alone" (DESIGN Colors; EXPERIENCE Component Patterns, Card color popover; CardTile.tsx). *Fix:* accessible name states the current category; show a glyph or letter on the tile.
